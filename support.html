Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my inspections saved?

Inspections are saved locally on your device.

Can I delete an inspection?

Yes. Open My Inspections, select the inspection you want to remove and choose Delete Inspection. Confirm the deletion.

Can I share an inspection report?

Yes. Car Checkr allows you to generate and share inspection reports using your device's available sharing options.

Does Car Checkr replace a mechanic?

No. Car Checkr is a guided inspection assistant and does not replace a qualified mechanic or professional pre-purchase vehicle inspection.

What if I cannot safely complete a check?

Skip the check and arrange professional inspection. Never perform an inspection that puts you at risk.
