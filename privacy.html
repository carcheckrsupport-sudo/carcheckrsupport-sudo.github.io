Car Checkr Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Car Checkr is a guided used-car inspection application. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Car Checkr allows you to enter and save:

  • Vehicle details, including registration and VIN
  • Vehicle listing information
  • Inspection responses and findings
  • Comments and inspection notes
  • Professional-verification records entered by users
  • Vehicle photographs
  • Inspection results and saved drafts

This information is stored locally on your device. The current app does not automatically upload inspection records to Car Checkr servers or a cloud database.

2. Analytics and Advertising

Car Checkr does not currently integrate third-party advertising, behavioural tracking, or app analytics services.

The app does not use inspection information for targeted advertising.

3. Photographs and Camera Access

Car Checkr may request access to your camera or photographs when you choose to add images to an inspection.

Inspection photographs are handled locally unless you choose to export or share them as part of a report.

4. Inspection Reports and Sharing

Car Checkr can generate inspection reports locally on your device.

If you choose to share a report using email, messaging, cloud storage, or another app, the selected service may receive and retain that information according to its own privacy practices.

Reports may contain vehicle identifiers, inspection findings, comments, photographs, and verification information.

5. Customer Support

If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address, message contents, and any information or attachments you choose to provide.

We use this information to respond to enquiries, investigate reported problems, and provide customer support.

Support correspondence is processed through email services, including Gmail. We do not sell customer support information.

6. Data Storage and Security

Inspection records are stored using local application storage and device files. Car Checkr relies on the security protections provided by your device's operating system.

The app does not currently provide a separate encryption layer for inspection records. No electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Deleting Information

You can delete saved inspections through the My Inspections section of the app. You can also discard unfinished inspections.

Deleting an inspection removes its saved record but may not remove separate drafts, temporary camera files, cached PDF reports, or copies previously exported or shared.

Information sent to third-party services may remain subject to those services' retention and deletion policies.

8. Third-Party Services and Website Hosting

Car Checkr uses software components from React Native and Expo to provide device functionality such as local storage, photography, PDF generation and sharing.

Apple and other service providers may separately process information when you use their operating systems, app stores, email services, or sharing destinations.

The Car Checkr website, Privacy Policy and Support pages are hosted using GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub.

When you visit these pages, GitHub may process technical information, including your IP address, for website hosting, security and operational purposes.

This processing is subject to GitHub's privacy practices. You can read the GitHub Privacy Statement for further information.

9. Children's Privacy

Car Checkr is intended for people assessing used vehicles. It is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly seek personal information from children through the app.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Car Checkr's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions, support enquiries, or requests concerning information provided through customer support, contact:
